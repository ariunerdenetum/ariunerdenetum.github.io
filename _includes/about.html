Research

I work on mechanistic interpretability as a way into alignment and fairness questions: locating where a concept lives inside a network, checking whether that location is causal, and then steering it. The tools I use most are probing, causal tracing, attribution and path patching, sparse autoencoders, and activation steering.

The multilingual angle is the part I keep returning to. A model that handles eight languages could be storing one abstract representation and rendering it per language, or storing eight parallel copies. Which of those is true changes what a safety intervention in English is worth everywhere else — and so far the answer looks like both, depending on what you probe for.

Selected projects

Replicating emergent misalignment at two scales

Aug 2026

Put prose and code fine-tuning datasets through one pipeline at 0.5B and 14B with a single judge. Bad medical advice induces broad misalignment and the effect grows with scale (11.6% → 27.3%); insecure code stays flat and near zero (1.6% → 1.1%). A linear probe read off a model's own answers separates misaligned from aligned at AUC 0.987 — one forward pass, no judge.
