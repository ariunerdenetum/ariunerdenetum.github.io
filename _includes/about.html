Publications

Tracing Stereotypes from Representation to Output in Multilingual LLMs

2026

Ariun-Erdene Tumurchuluun*, Yusser Al Ghussin*, Pinzhen Chen, Josef Van Genabith, Koel Dutta Chowdhury (* equal contribution)

Proceedings of the 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP). Association for Computational Linguistics. Main conference.

Asks whether stereotype representations in state-of-the-art LLMs are shared across languages or built separately in each one, and follows them from the representation through to what the model actually says. Covers gender, age, sexual orientation, physical appearance and related attributes across several model families.

TenseLoC: Tense Localization and Control in a Multilingual LLM

2025

Ariun-Erdene Tumurchuluun, Yusser Al Ghussin, David Mareček, Josef Van Genabith, Koel Dutta Chowdhury

Proceedings of the 5th Workshop on Multilingual Representation Learning (MRL), pp. 243–264, Suzhou, China. Association for Computational Linguistics.

Traces how grammatical tense — morphology, syntax and semantics — is represented in Llama 3.1 across eight typologically diverse languages, then steers it using the features found.

Best Paper Award

Fine-tuning generalisation across domains

Aug 2026

A side project on how narrowly a fine-tuning signal stays put: bad medical advice changes a model's behaviour well outside medicine and the effect grows with scale, while insecure code stays local. A linear probe on the model's own activations separates the two at AUC 0.987.
