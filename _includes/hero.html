{{ site.author.location }} · also goes by Ariuka

I study what large language models actually represent inside, and how those representations shape what the models say. Most of my work asks a cross-lingual version of that question: when an LLM encodes something — grammatical tense, or a stereotype about age or gender — is that representation shared across languages, or rebuilt separately in each one?

  • M.Sc. in Language & Communication Technologies, an Erasmus Mundus double degree from Charles University and Saarland University.
  • Best Paper Award at MRL @ EMNLP 2025 for localizing and steering tense in a multilingual LLM.
  • Currently finishing work on whether stereotype bias is universal or language-specific across model families — under review at EMNLP ARR.
  • Most recently replicated emergent misalignment at 0.5B and 14B, and built a judge-free probe that detects it — write-up and interactive demo.
  • Before research: three years as a full-stack and AI engineer in Ulaanbaatar.
  • Applying for PhD positions in interpretability and AI safety.
